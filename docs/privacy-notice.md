# Privacy notice

*Draft for review before launch. Check it against what the app actually does, and name
who to contact — the site has no operator name yet.*

## What we collect

**Your account.** Name, email address, phone number, and your student ID, branch and year.
We check the PEC student email domain when it is set, so accounts belong to students.

**Your profile.** A short bio, skills, interests, and optionally a profile photo and a
resume (PDF).

**What you apply to.** Which club and opening you applied to, the answers you wrote to that
club's questions, and the status of your application.

**How you use the site.** Chat conversations with the AI assistant are stored so you can
come back to them. They are deleted automatically after 180 days.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not track you across other websites.

## Who can see what

| Your data | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| Name, email, branch, year, photo | Any signed-in student (it is how applications are shown to clubs) |
| Your answers to a club's questions | That club's admins, and the super admin |
| The status of your application | You, that club's admins, the super admin |
| Your resume | The clubs you apply to, and the super admin |
| Your phone number | The admins of clubs you apply to, and the super admin |
| Chats with the AI assistant | You, and the super admin can see aggregate usage and cost, not your conversations |

Club admins see the applicants for their own club only. They cannot see other clubs'
applicants. The site's technical administrator can see all data in order to fix faults and
moderate abuse.

## AI features and Anthropic

The answer checker, the mock interview bot, the help assistant, and the optional AI summary
a club admin sees of an applicant all send text to **Anthropic**, the company that makes the
Claude models. That text can include:

- the question you are answering and your answer
- your profile (branch, year, skills, bio)
- your applications, when you ask the assistant about them
- a club's public description

Only your **own** data is ever sent, never another student's. The AI features are marked
in the interface. You can turn off the AI summary in your profile settings, and you can
withdraw your consent to AI processing at any time; without it the AI features are
disabled and the rest of the site works normally.

## Cookies

One cookie, `token`, keeps you signed in. It is `HttpOnly` (JavaScript cannot read it),
`SameSite=Lax`, and `Secure` in production. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

## How long we keep things

- Chats with the AI assistant: **180 days**, then deleted automatically.
- Your account and applications: until you delete your account.
- Backups: database snapshots are kept for **14 days** and then deleted.

## Your rights

You can, from your profile:

- **Export everything** — a single JSON file of your account, your applications and your
  chats.
- **Delete your account.** Your profile, photo, resume, phone number and chats are deleted
  immediately.

When you delete your account, the applications you sent are **kept** but stripped of your
name, email, phone number and answers, because the club that received them needs a record
of its recruitment. They cannot reconnect those applications to you.

You can also ask the site's operator to correct or delete your data by email.

## Children

This site is for Punjab Engineering College students. It is not directed at children under
16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this notice changes materially, the change will be announced on the site before it takes
effect.
